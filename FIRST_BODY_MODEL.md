### 11.4 Body-local truth and currentness conflict

`HYPOTHESIS · PRESSURE-SUPPORTED`

The Body must be able to answer four different questions without forcing them into one state claim:

| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Normative | What is locally binding, authorized, applicable, or committed? |
| Actual | What materially or relationally happened or presently exists? |
| Epistemic | What can presently be supported as known, with what uncertainty? |
| Deliberative | What is being proposed, interpreted, simulated, or considered? |

These dimensions may truthfully diverge. An unauthorized effect may alter actual conditions without changing normative Authority. A proposal may remain deliberative even when confidently summarized. Uncertainty about causation or validity belongs in epistemic state rather than being repaired through inference.

A supportable Body-state claim keeps recoverable:

1. the exact local target and claimed condition;
2. the valid local basis, actual impact, or evidence supporting the relevant dimension;
3. whether the intended or observed effect became real;
4. when the claim became applicable and whether it was superseded;
5. any material conflict or uncertainty.

When carriers disagree, first distinguish their target, dimension, and time; then trace each claim to its basis and effect. A maintainer may repair a stale representation when the underlying local state is unambiguous. It may not choose between competing normative bases, treat newer prose as a local decision, or resolve ambiguity in favour of its own power. ### 11.5 Local temporality and truthful resumption

`HYPOTHESIS · PRESSURE-SUPPORTED`

- when it entered Body-local deliberation or knowledge;
- when it acquired local effect or applicability;
- which earlier occurrence or relation it depended upon;
- whether its exact order relative to another event remains `UNRESOLVED`.

Dormancy does not freeze external reality. The last supported pre-gap state remains historical evidence of what stood before interruption; it does not automatically describe the present after return. On resumption, carried reality separates into:

1. irreversible historical occurrences;
2. relations, commitments, law, and Authority whose continuation must be resolved through their own lifecycles;
3. actual changes or impacts that arose during the gap without requiring local permission;
4. unresolved claims that neither memory, freshness, nor fluent reconstruction may complete by confidence.

A same-Body continuity claim minimally requires recoverable support for:

1. the formed Body and occurrence through which it came into existence;
2. its bounded locality or Locus;
3. its locally applicable law and relevant continuation or termination conditions;

6. the absence of a supported cessation, replacement, fork, or fulfilled termination condition that defeats the claim.

This support may be distributed across evidence, testimony, carriers, occurrences, and external facts; no sacred file is required. Missing evidence does not prove cessation, but it may leave present continuity `UNRESOLVED`. Copying state-bearing Material creates another carrier, not another Body, fork, successor, or continuation claim by possession.

Multiple passive or byte-identical carriers may therefore preserve evidence and recoverability for one dormant Body. Activation alone still does not multiply Body identity: several executions may remain interfaces, organs, deliberative environments, or redundant processes inside one Body when their effects resolve through one Body-local currentness and Authority relation.

Independent consequential divergence changes the problem. If two executions begin from a shared state and admit incompatible consequences through separate relations, two factual occurrence streams now exist. Shared provenance remains true, but neither technical priority nor byte ancestry determines whether one stream is the supported continuation, both are new Bodies with common ancestry, one is an unsupported identity claim, or a pre-existing distributed-Body topology lawfully carries both. Where the relevant formation, Authority, or topology cannot be reconstructed, classification remains `UNRESOLVED`.

The minimum non-conversion rules are:

```text
carrier multiplicity != Body multiplicity
state replication != jurisdiction replication
shared history != shared currentness after incompatible consequence
technical branch != constitutional successor
```

A singular Body may carry internal plurality only through an actual topology capable of holding it. Divergent carriers cannot silently preserve one singular currentness merely because they share a predecessor image, and a branch does not inherit jurisdiction by lineage.

The architecture must keep four questions distinct:

| Question | Required relation |
| --- | --- |
| Did this Body exist? | Historical formation and identity |
| Does the same Body continue? | Supported continuity through the gap |
| Can it presently resume? | Sufficient current reconstruction and a lawful route to motion |
| Who may act now? | Presently exercisable bearer–function Authority |

Recoverability therefore does not prove continuity; continuity does not supply resumability; resumability does not assign present Authority. Elapsed time or silence alone does not perform cessation, while absence of an explicit cessation record does not guarantee endless continuation where an actual termination condition or irrecoverable loss may have occurred.

A replacement human, DI, provider, implementation, or carrier receives access, capability, evidence, or a possible function only through its supported present relation. It does not inherit the predecessor's identity, memory, Authority, authorization, or interpretation by replacement alone. Authorization must therefore distinguish whether it attaches to an exact bearer, a function, a qualifying execution relation, or an exact combination, and must expose any replacement conditions.

Truthful resumption requires enough recoverable basis to:

These are truth conditions, not five ceremonies or mandatory documents. One compact occurrence may carry several or all of them. The architectural compression is:

> The Body does not resume where it stopped. It resumes from what remains true after the gap has been admitted.

Preserved history alone therefore supports reconstruction but does not complete resumption. Runtime uptime is not constitutional life; truthful re-entry into current reality and renewed answerable motion are the stronger test.
