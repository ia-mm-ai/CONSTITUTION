# CONTINUITY STATE CAPABILITY

## Standing and subject

**Structural placeholder.** Read with [STATE](../STATE.md) and the
[Constitution](../../SOURCE/CONSTITUTION_0%28%291.md), especially Article 4,
§§ 14.3–14.5, Articles 15–17, and §§ 19.3–19.4.

Continuity State Capability (CSC) is demonstrated effective ability, under
stated conditions, to preserve supported State succession across transition,
dormancy, correction, and resumption (§ 16.7). This directory reserves the
subjects of that account; it does not itself demonstrate CSC.

## Boundaries retained

- State, its representation, and its record remain distinct (§ 14.5).
  Actual consequences remain visible even where the underlying act was
  invalid (§§ 14.1–14.4).
- Correction preserves predecessor truth; it does not erase an occurrence
  (§§ 15.3–15.5).
- Dormancy is not ending or identity loss. Replacement restores no inherited
  identity, consent, Agency, autonomy, or Authority (§§ 16.1–16.5).
- Incompatible consequential streams remain visibly distinct, conflicted, or
  validly reconciled (§ 16.6).
- State succession does not establish succession at every other layer.
  [LINEAGE](../LINEAGE/LINEAGE.md) reserves the exact target, independent
  basis, successor-side establishment, carry map, and effective boundary
  required by §§ 17.9–17.15.
- [DCR](../DYNAMIC-CAPACITY-REGULATION/DYNAMIC-CAPACITY-REGULATION.md) concerns
  presently supportable motion; preserved succession cannot retroactively
  establish capacity or Authority (§ 19.4).

## Reserved qualification

A future CSC claim must identify the exact bearer or mechanism, scope,
conditions, demonstration, dependencies, failure boundaries, and present
status (§ 16.8). No such claim is made here. A future embodiment remains
distinct from the capability it claims to demonstrate; no particular
technology is prescribed (§ 19.3).
