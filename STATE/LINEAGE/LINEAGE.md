# LINEAGE

## Standing and subject

**Structural placeholder.** Read with [STATE](../STATE.md) and the
[Constitution](../../SOURCE/CONSTITUTION_0%28%291.md), especially §§ 1.2–1.5,
§§ 6.4–6.9, Articles 9 and 16–20.

Lineage makes attributable occurrences and exact relations referenceable
without converting ancestry, resemblance, custody, copying, or later
appearance into succession. The repository is a source/origin reference,
not a parent jurisdiction or an authority over external formations.

defined constitutional categories. Re-entry does not presume resumption of a
Body, renewed Presence, or completed succession. Derivation does not decide
whether a constitutional change is adaptation, amendment, transformation, or
new formation; Article 17 supplies those distinctions.

## Source and origin

`ORIGIN.json` locates the existing constitutional forms and records the exact
human-file byte binding already carried by the machine Form. The digest
identifies bytes only: it establishes no author identity, first occurrence,
adoption, Authority, current Source availability, or worldwide priority.
The locator does not claim a genesis event or fabricate predecessor history.

## Boundaries retained

- Source attribution is not ownership; historical attribution is not
  continuing availability. Source never transfers through copying, custody,
  or succession (§§ 6.4–6.9).
- A local relation record does not establish the other side's reciprocal
  relation or merge the related terms (§§ 9.2–9.5).
- Succession requires an exact target and a basis valid apart from the
  successor's claim. Designation alone does not establish the successor-side
  relation (§§ 17.9–17.11).
- A carry map distinguishes what continues, ends, validly crosses, remains
  predecessor-only, is refused/excluded/unknown/unresolved, or newly begins
  with the successor. What is not expressly and validly carried does not
  cross; Source must be newly attributable (§ 17.12).
- Effective boundaries, predecessor remainder, failures, consequences, and
  answerability remain recoverable (§§ 17.13–17.15).
- A mechanism's claim to embody
  [CSC](../CONTINUITY-STATE-CAPABILITY/CONTINUITY-STATE-CAPABILITY.md) or
  [DCR](../DYNAMIC-CAPACITY-REGULATION/DYNAMIC-CAPACITY-REGULATION.md) is not
  the function, its constitutional source, or proof of general conformance.
  Demonstration and dependencies remain exact and bounded (§§ 16.8, 18.4,
  19.3–19.4, 20.2).
