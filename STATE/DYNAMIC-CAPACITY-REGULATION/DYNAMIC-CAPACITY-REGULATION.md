# DYNAMIC CAPACITY REGULATION

## Standing and subject

**Structural placeholder.** Read with [STATE](../STATE.md) and the
[Constitution](../../SOURCE/CONSTITUTION_0%28%291.md), especially Article 4,
Articles 10–13, Article 18, and §§ 19.3–19.4.

Dynamic Capacity Regulation (DCR) is the local, time-sensitive reconciliation
of structural capacity with situated capacity (§ 13.7). What a Form leaves
possible is not what an exact Locality can presently bear, sustain, or
exercise. This directory reserves their distinct representation, not a
universal permission mechanism or a claim of actual capacity.

## Boundaries retained

- Agency, autonomy, capability, structural capacity, situated capacity,
  regulation, Authority, execution, and consequence remain separately
  evaluated for the exact motion (Article 11).
- Support for capacity is not an authoritative permission. Each necessary
  relation requires its own sufficient basis; not every motion requires the
  same composition (§§ 11.10–11.13).
- HOLD preserves an exact condition without deciding it. A materially
  constraining HOLD remains attributable, scoped, reviewable, correctable,
  and capable of ending (§§ 13.3–13.4).
- Refusal identifies its target and effect; scoped failure is not general
  incapacity or exclusion (§§ 11.12, 13.2).
- Evidence supports only its declared object and ceiling. Technical results
  do not establish private conditions, adoption, or legitimacy (Article 18,
  Reading 7).
- [CSC](../CONTINUITY-STATE-CAPABILITY/CONTINUITY-STATE-CAPABILITY.md) concerns
  supported succession, not the present reconciliation of capacity.
  [LINEAGE](../LINEAGE/LINEAGE.md) preserves relations without providing a
  missing basis for motion (§ 19.4).

No thresholds, scoring model, global disposition vocabulary, mechanism, or
privileged embodiment are specified here. Further specification must preserve
these distinctions without adding constitutional meaning (Readings 3–6).
