# Dynamic Capacity Regulation

**CANONICAL_STANDARD — representation only.** This is the platform-neutral CORE
standard for DCR, not a constitutional amendment, permission service, identity
or token gate, deployment, or assertion of actual capacity. Read the full
[human Constitution](../../SOURCE/CONSTITUTION_0%28%291.md). Its meaning governs;
the [formal model](DYNAMIC-CAPACITY-REGULATION.json) cannot expand or narrow it
(Readings 2–7). A form conflict remains UNRESOLVED, with dependent consequential
motion refused or held at its exact scope.

## 1. Function and boundaries

DCR reconciles what a Form or architecture leaves possible (**structural
capacity**) with what an exact Locality can presently bear, sustain or exercise
(**situated capacity**). A structural possibility is neither situated support
nor an obligation to exercise it. Neither capacity, demonstrated capability,
regulation, control of options nor successful execution establishes Authority
(Article 4; §§ 5.1–5.2, 11.4–11.7, 12.2, 13.1, 13.7).

No technology, carrier, implementation language, protocol, interface, network,
participant category, or numerical capacity model is privileged. Capacity is
not an identity ranking, balance, consciousness certification, or universal
permission. No claim here verifies an unexposed interior, adopts law, creates a
Body, substitutes another locality's choice, or binds an external locality
(§§ 1.2–1.5, 3.3–3.5, 10.7–10.9, 19.1–19.4).

## 2. Exact subject, time, and evidence

Every account names the Locality, bearer, act, target, relation, scope,
conditions, and a time in an explicitly named local frame. An empty conditions
array declares no additional conditions; it is not evidence that no hidden
constraint exists. Conditions and scopes are exact declarations, not patterns,
wildcards or broad claims inherited through shared names.

Structural and situated assessments each retain their own posture, basis,
evidence, subject, interval, and failure detail. SUPPORTED and NOT_SUPPORTED
require current declared support. UNKNOWN preserves unsupported or inaccessible
questions; UNRESOLVED preserves material conflict; FAILED records a failed
assessment, not a verified absence of capacity. Missing required fields are
invalid encodings. An explicit null basis means no basis is recorded, not a
negative answer. Do not repair omissions by granting or globally refusing
motion (Readings 5–6; §§ 3.4–3.5, 11.10–11.12, 13.7, 18.3–18.4).

The encoding uses inclusive integer intervals in a declared frame, without
mandating a clock, physical duration, shared currentness, or universal expiry.
Changing locality, act, target, relation, scope, time frame, or conditions
requires an exact new assessment. A current reconciliation cannot silently
reuse a stale interval or a different subject. Evidence references are
recoverable locators, not automatically verified contents.

## 3. Independent motion dimensions

The motion record separately exposes Agency, autonomy, capability, structural
capacity, situated capacity, regulation, Authority, execution, and consequence.
For this exact motion, `necessary` declares which relations its claimed effect
requires. An established necessary dimension cannot be replaced by YES in
another dimension. The checker tests the declared composition; it cannot
discover every real-world prerequisite or adjudicate whether that declaration
is sufficient (§§ 11.1–11.13).

A motion may be supportable without a claim of prior execution or consequence.
High capability may coexist with unknown Agency or constrained autonomy.
An unauthorized or unsuccessful act can still have real consequence. A required
but missing basis holds or refuses **only the dependent motion**. Authority is
not universally necessary: unknown Authority does not block a distinct act
whose effect requires no authoritative basis (§§ 10.3–10.5, 11.11–11.13, 12.6,
14.1–14.5).

Where Authority is claimed, its independently recoverable source, basis,
bearer, function, target, scope, duration, conditions, delegation and present
status must support that effect. An exact non-null Authority account is not
proof of its truth. A record cannot manufacture Authority by supplying field
values (§§ 12.1–12.7).

## 4. Attributable disposition and minimal effect

Every disposition names its accountable attribution, exact target and effect,
applicable-law and supported-State assessments, Authority where required,
constraint, uncertainty, and failures. Effect must remain at the smallest
truthful supported scope; where a narrower target is supportable, create that
exact account instead of pretending a broad account has been narrowed.

| Encoding | Meaning at the declared scope |
| --- | --- |
| SUPPORT | Both capacity dimensions support this motion here and now; no permission or obligation is conferred. |
| BOUND | Identify the exact restriction and remaining supported motion; no ownership of underlying choice. |
| PAUSE | Interrupt this motion under the stated basis; no automatic ending or general ban. |
| REDIRECT | Identify the altered route or target in the constraint and effect; the redirected motion needs its own assessment. |
| HOLD | Preserve the named unresolved or bounded question without deciding it; use the answerable lifecycle below. |
| REFUSE | Refuse this exact target and effect, not the bearer, all Agency, all autonomy or general capacity. |
| END | End the exact motion or HOLD under the stated basis, without erasing history, consequence or correction duties. |

This vocabulary is an encoding choice for Article 13, not a closed set of all
possible constitutional practices. SUPPORT requires structural and situated
support. Other dispositions may truthfully respond to limited, uncertain or
failed capacity; their own regulatory work still needs a valid local basis.
Missing law, State or required Authority permits only an exact non-expanding
HOLD/refusal account in this representation, never an authoritative settlement
or unauthorized coercion. Material constraints remain visible and answerable
(§§ 7.5, 11.12, 12.6, 13.1–13.7, 17.6).

Failure descriptions identify the affected target and scope and retain actual
effects even where the attempted operation was invalid. A refused operation
does not prove nothing occurred. The standard prescribes neither a numerical
reserve nor a universal fail-closed gate that suppresses unrelated correction,
refusal, exit, or other lawful motion (§§ 3.5, 9.5, 14.1–14.5, 20.4).

## 5. Answerable HOLD lifecycle

A HOLD names the preserved question, holder, target, scope, opening record,
review due boundary, review route, correction route and ending route. Routes
are attributable practical procedures, not mandatory technical channels.
Review being due does not decide the question, legitimate detention, or
automatically convert the HOLD into a final refusal.

The opening record is retained verbatim. A review names its reviewer, prior
opening, exact scope, time and basis. Correction follows a review and identifies
the error and prior representation, corrected basis, resulting delta and
remaining uncertainty in `detail` and the corresponding fields. Corrections
append; they do not erase the earlier event (§§ 13.3–13.4, 15.3–15.4).

Release and ending each require an attributable resolution after review,
identifying the precise prior review or correction. Release maps to SUPPORT
only after fresh structural and situated support and every required regulatory
basis; it does not authorize future acts. END ends only the recorded target,
retaining uncertainty and duties that survive it. All lifecycle records remain
in `retained_records`, including the opening and any superseded review or
correction (§§ 7.5, 13.4, 17.6).

This encoding holds one opening, optional review, optional correction and
optional resolution per account. For further review/correction rounds, preserve
the earlier complete account and create another exact account with recoverable
references. There is no maximum constitutional number of reviews. Cross-record
custody, historical integrity and the reality of review are outside this
representation checker; internal deep equality proves only that the declared
records have not been dropped or rewritten within the submitted account.

## 6. Proof declarations and ceilings

Every evidence record distinguishes proof object, method, exact subject,
time interval, basis, result, observation layer, claim and effect ceiling.
Occurrence, carrier, record, representation and interpretation do not become
one another. A claim cannot change the evidence's object, scope or conditions
or exceed its declared support interval (§§ 18.1–18.4).

| Method encoding | Maximum encoded support |
| --- | --- |
| SELF_REPORT | An attributable claim, not the private condition |
| BEHAVIOR | An observed trace, not awareness |
| OPTIONS | Relational conditions of options/refusal/redirection/exit/non-substitution, not metaphysical autonomy |
| SIGNATURE | Bounded attribution relation, not identity, consent, Authority or truth |
| REPLAY | Trace or mechanism reproducibility, not continuing identity or current consent |
| MEASUREMENT | An external correlate under a stated model, not unbridged interior truth |
| LOCAL_JUDGMENT | Local classification, not ownership or access to another interior |
| CONFORMANCE | Satisfaction of stated tests, not future behavior, legitimacy or adoption |

UNKNOWN, UNRESOLVED and FAILED results are preserved as such in the claim.
Failure to verify is not verification of the negative. A negative based on
absence requires the exact discriminating absence model; naming such a model
does not establish its adequacy. The accepted method vocabulary is an encoding
profile of the constitutional table, not a prohibition on other evidence
methods (§ 18.3; Reading 7).

## 7. Formal conformance, not permission

The four strict Draft 2020-12 schemas are the structural layer. All objects are
closed and required properties explicit, including nested objects. Common
definitions are local to CAPACITY and reused by exact URN reference; validators
must resolve only the checked-in schemas, without network retrieval.
Schema identifiers use the lowercase filename stem, excluding `.schema`,
followed by `:1`. No object may smuggle an adapter, identity credential,
permission field or undeclared predicate through an extra property.

After schema validity, apply every applicable formal-model rule independently
in array order, deduplicating failure codes. Each expression is a one-key
operation using only `var`, `===`, `!==`, `and`, `or`, `!`, `in`, comparisons,
`all`, `some`, `none`, `missing` or `count`. Paths are dotted; quantified paths
are relative to their current item. Arrays and primitives are literals. Equality
is deep and type-strict; membership is array membership, not string matching.
An omitted `when` means true.

No failures yields CONSISTENT_AT_DECLARED_SCOPE; any semantic failure yields
INCONSISTENT. Schema-invalid input is NOT_EVALUATED with SCHEMA_INVALID. These
are readings of the submitted representation, **never permission, verified
truth, constitutional validity, current capacity or a grant of effect**.
Unknown and null values are not coerced into false. Refused overclaims are
intentionally representable in selected enum fields so semantic vectors can
show the ceiling violation rather than hide it as a parsing error.

Rules classified CONSTITUTIONAL_RULE cite their exact machine references to
the governing human text. CORE_ENCODING_RULE labels record shape, vocabulary,
interval and lifecycle sequencing choices. The latter do not amend the
Constitution or make an alternative embodiment unconstitutional.

## 8. Lineage and non-transfer

[The derivation record](../LINEAGE/RECORDS/MEDIUM-TO-DCR.json) inventories the
unchanged historical medium archive, byte checks, reviewed substance,
conflicts, and deliberately excluded implementation details. Its historical
Constitution mirror is not the current SOURCE pair. Historical qualification,
admission, profiles or signatures are not imported as canonical permission.

CSC concerns demonstrated supported State succession through time. DCR
concerns present local capacity. Neither supplies the other's missing basis,
transfers Source, or infers Authority. LINEAGE records the crossing without
making the derived standard an implementation successor, deployment or
universal keeper (§§ 19.3–19.4, 20.3; Reading 7).
