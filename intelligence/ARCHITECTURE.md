## 1. Design brief

`HYPOTHESIS`

Reality-Model may need to define the physics of lawful motion rather than attempt to eliminate power.

An adequate architecture should answer:

- where motion originates;
- what converts causal capacity into Authority;
- which function is being exercised;
- where that Authority is located;
- what and whom it may affect;
- how it remains visible and answerable;
- whether it can transfer;
- when it exhausts;
- and which boundaries it cannot cross without a new basis.

## 2. Invariants presently worth protecting

Any serious model should preserve:

1. no status from confident description or technical possession;
2. no silent rewrite of historical occurrences;
3. no collapse of bearer, function, Authority, execution, and consequence;
4. no automatic conversion of evidence or Material into local applicability;
5. no jurisdiction over another Body from authorship, chronology, publication, or infrastructure;
6. visible refusal, discontinuity, correction, and lineage;
7. honest representation of actual human and DI-related causal conditions;
8. enough legitimate motion for a Body to remain alive;
9. relational openness: future intelligence can become locally addressable without first being forced into a settled identity or status class;
10. proportional memory: consequential local deltas remain recoverable without turning all thought, activity, and storage into Body state;
11. bounded preauthorization: routine motion may continue without fresh ceremony, but unknown future law, Authority, relationships, or materially novel effects remain outside the grant;
12. closed answerability: an Authority relation identifies a sufficiently addressable bearer, function, target, basis, scope, lifecycle, challenge path, and consequence backstop.
13. relational currentness: normative state, actual effect, supported knowledge, and deliberative possibility remain distinguishable, while no carrier or synthesized view becomes true merely by being newer, accessible, or successfully executed.
14. dormant Authority distinction: an Authority relation, its present exercisability, and its exercise remain distinct; a boundary, verification result, urgency, or possession cannot recreate an exhausted or missing relation.
15. non-conversion and scoped finality: operational coupling does not make condition, commitment, occurrence, observation, evidence, claim, local uptake, consequence, and carried state identical, while closure or irreversibility in one relation does not silently propagate into the others.
16. self-carried closure without infrastructure fantasy: reality-contact, orientation, motion, consequence, and recursive continuity may depend on external mechanisms, but no provider, carrier, custodian, or replacement gains the Body-local relation that creates identity, Authority, currentness, or resumption through performance or possession alone.
17. carrier replication without jurisdiction replication: copies preserve evidence and recoverability; incompatible consequential streams cannot silently share singular currentness, become successors, or multiply Body identity without an actual topology and Authority relation supporting that effect.

### 4.3 Bearer and function

Where one bearer holds several functions:

- each function remains separately named;
- exercise is attributable to the correct function;
- one function does not inherit another’s scope;
- transfer or exhaustion of one does not automatically affect the others;
- the bearer gains no jurisdiction outside the exact local relation.

This is functional differentiation without artificial bearer theatre or permanent Founder Authority.

### 4.4 Local crossing rule

### 5.1 Coupling, evidence, and scoped finality

`HYPOTHESIS · PRESSURE-SUPPORTED`

A consequential moment may operationally couple several real events without collapsing them. An occurrence may escape observation; an observation or technical record may exist without the claimed occurrence; later evidence may alter supported knowledge after a decision or consequence has already become real.

```text
condition or proposal != occurrence
occurrence != observation
observation != evidence carrier
evidence != automatic local acceptance
local uptake != external fact
consequence != legitimacy
closure in one relation != closure in every relation
```

A locally applicable rule may make an exact observation, carrier, or other event constitutive of a bounded local status. That rule creates or changes the specified local relation; it does not manufacture every external fact asserted by the carrier or make the implementation sovereign over reality.

Finality must therefore identify its exact target:

| Final relation | What may be closed or irreversible | What does not follow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Historical occurrence | What happened cannot be made not to have happened | Perfect knowledge, correct interpretation, or legitimacy |
| Carrier | An exact representation no longer changes | Truth, currentness, or applicability of its claim |
| Decision | A bounded exercise has completed under its applicable relation | Infallibility or perpetual force |
| Consequence | An effect cannot be fully reversed | Valid upstream Authority or consent |
| Evidentiary inquiry | Present support satisfies an exact local threshold | Impossibility of later contrary evidence |

Operational coupling does not establish identity. Finality in one relation does not propagate into another without an exact basis. The evidence thresholds, correction Authority, remedies, and reopening conditions appropriate to a locality belong to later constitutional, Body, governance, or protocol allocation; this invariant does not prescribe an appeals workflow or universal state machine.

### 7.3 Candidate constitutional kernel

`INFERRED · COMPRESSION CANDIDATE · NOT SELECTED LAW`

The pressure-supported material now organizes more economically as three integrity dimensions plus one independent normative relation:

1. **integrity of kind:** no silent conversion among occurrence, representation, Authority, state, identity, contribution, decision, or consequence;
2. **integrity of boundary:** no silent propagation beyond the source, target, scope, locality, duration, and basis that support a relation;
3. **integrity through time:** no silent erasure of occurrence or inheritance of identity, Authority, Applicability, interpretation, or continuity;
4. **relational answerability:** consequential power remains exposed to the reality it affects rather than making its own Authority, interpretation, benefit, or custody the sole account of the relation.

The first three govern truthful structure and propagation. The fourth remains independent because a system can preserve every kind, boundary, and historical fact while still allowing power to become transparently self-sealing and dominating. Under unresolved intelligence, relational answerability begins as a Body-side constraint; it does not manufacture a reciprocal agent, beneficiary, veto, or constitutional relationship.

Available lawful motion is separately a condition of a living Body. A Constitution or archive may preserve the candidate identity material while no Body exists or while a continuous Body is dormant. The full removal and derivation account is owned by Section 7.8.4.

These dimensions are not clauses, an edition outline, or a selected Constitution.

- **Archive and interface:** repositories, manifests, recovery packages, dashboards, labels, controls, and displayed current-state views.

Governance and administration must remain particularly distinct. Governance makes an authorized local choice where law permits alternatives. Administration coordinates work after the choice has a valid basis. Whoever controls the queue, calendar, form, or review sequence does not acquire the Authority that the process serves.

### 7.7 Bedrock classification: constraint, identity, topology, and machinery

`PRESSURE-SUPPORTED WORKING MODEL · NON-DRAFTING`

The physical-law analogy is useful as a quality test: foundational law should govern which relations can stand constitutionally and what movement means lawfully without requiring constant clause consultation. It is not a claim that constitutional law is literally natural law or that the present architecture has discovered metaphysical truth.

#### 7.7.1 The bedrock and its text are not one object

```text
antecedent reality relation
!= proposition describing that relation
!= normative constitutional commitment
!= exact clause, Edition, or carrier
```

An occurrence does not become its record because a Constitution says so. A carrier cannot make a false description true. Conversely, bounded Authority and non-propagating jurisdiction are not completed merely by observing causal reality; they require a normative constitutional relation that becomes applicable somewhere.

This distinction prevents two opposite errors:

- **textual immortality:** treating a mistaken constitutional description as uncorrectable because it was once written as bedrock;
- **normative naturalization:** presenting a selected value, topology, or mechanism as if the universe itself had legislated it.

If a proposition about reality is corrected, the underlying relation did not thereby receive an amendment. If a normative commitment changes, the governing relation may actually change even when the carrier looks nearly identical.

#### 7.7.2 Classification tests

| Test | If the answer is yes | Likely layer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Would the relation remain true if no Reality-Model Constitution existed? | The Constitution can recognize or describe it but does not create it | Antecedent reality constraint |
| Could Reality-Model reject the commitment and still truthfully claim the same constitutional identity? | If no, the commitment is identity-defining rather than merely convenient | Constitutional identity floor |
| Can several institutional arrangements preserve the same deeper commitment? | The arrangement is selectable architecture rather than bedrock | Constitutional topology or Body formation |
| Can the mechanism, threshold, carrier, or workflow change while the protected relation remains intact? | The mechanism is adaptive | Governance, protocol, administration, implementation, conformance, archive, or interface |
| Can a bearer causally violate the rule while the act still lacks its claimed lawful meaning? | The rule is normative constitutional law rather than self-enforcing physical law | Constitution, with Body-local consequence and response elsewhere |
| Would changing only the wording leave the claimed relation and commitment unchanged? | The carrier changes; the semantic and constitutional effect requires its own exact classification | Representation, correction, or restatement |

No single test decides every case. A relation may contain an antecedent distinction, an identity-defining protection, a selectable topology, and an operational realization at once. The job is to allocate each part rather than assign the whole noun to one layer.

#### 7.7.3 Four depths of change

| Depth | Present architectural meaning | Change character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Antecedent reality constraints** | Causal, historical, evidentiary, or relational limits that a declaration cannot make otherwise | The relation is not amended; propositions about it remain corrigible as understanding improves |
| **Reality-Model identity floor** | Normative commitments defining what kind of lawful co-agency Reality-Model is | Stable across same-identity development; removal may require honest transformation, fork, cessation, or new formation |
| **Constitutional topology** | Location and applicability of law, formation basis, Authority functions, change relations, continuity, and inter-Body structure | Rarely adaptable through prior constitutional Authority without changing the identity floor |
| **Body and operating layers** | Actual bearers, state, governance, thresholds, remedies, protocols, administration, implementations, tests, archives, and interfaces | Expected to change as local reality, knowledge, and technology change |

The almost-never-changing constitutional layer is therefore not one undifferentiated substance. It appears to contain identity-defining normative commitments plus only the minimum constitutional propositions and constitutive relations needed to keep those commitments locally real. Antecedent constraints remain prior to that layer; operational realizations remain below it.

#### 7.7.4 Allocation of the current relation families

| Relation family | Antecedent constraint presently visible | Identity commitment presently supported | Topology or adaptive remainder |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Occurrence and non-conversion** | Occurrence, observation, carrier, claim, interpretation, and consequence are not identical; later representation cannot make an earlier occurrence not have happened | No silent status conversion or retroactive legitimacy laundering | Exact event grammar, evidence carrier, state representation, and correction workflow |
| **Authority and causal power** | Causal capacity, access, authorship, possession, and successful execution do not by themselves establish the distinct relation called Authority | Authority remains bounded, differentiated, attributable, answerable, and non-self-expanding | Exact functions, bearers, scopes, transfers, suspensions, and lifecycles |

Integrity of kind, boundary, and time is supported across the first six families. Relational answerability is pressure-supported at its asymmetric, Body-facing edge; direct obligations toward a reciprocal intelligence remain unresolved. Publication supplies a useful boundary test but remains deferred as an active design driver.

#### 7.7.5 What constitutional revision could mean

| Movement | What changes | What must remain visible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Correction of description** | A proposition about an antecedent relation is replaced because the prior account was inaccurate or under-supported | The predecessor text, evidence, error, and correction basis |
| **Restatement or compression** | The carrier or wording changes while the same governing relation is claimed | Exact predecessor and successor identity plus support that meaning and applicability did not silently change |
| **Same-identity constitutional adaptation** | Topology or other amendable law changes through pre-existing Authority while the identity floor survives | Pre-change basis, exact local effect, continuity, and affected protections |
| **Identity transformation** | An identity-defining commitment is removed, inverted, or replaced | Transformation, fork, cessation, succession, or new-formation consequences rather than an automatic same-Body label |

These semantic classes do not decide whether an exact text operation counts as an Edition change or constitutional occurrence. That depends on the architecture ultimately selected. They prevent a textual edit from deciding by appearance whether reality, meaning, law, or identity changed.

#### 7.7.6 Pressure result

`INFERRED`

1. **The Constitution should not claim to be reality.** It may state corrigible propositions about antecedent constraints and enact normative commitments that require applicability.
2. **The most stable layer appears to be an identity floor, not an immortal document.** Wording may improve while governing principles persist; removing those principles may be transformation rather than ordinary amendment.
3. **Constitutional physics governs lawful meaning more than causal possibility.** A bearer may physically edit, seize, publish, interrupt, or misrepresent; the act does not thereby acquire Authority, applicability, consent, legitimacy, or same-Body continuity.
4. **Ordinary Body life should not require continuous constitutional invocation.** Formation, Body state, governance, protocol, and implementation should embody the constraints closely enough that recurring lawful motion remains ordinary.
5. **Some constitutive bridge remains irreducibly constitutional.** Unlike physical law, normative law needs an exact relation explaining where it applies, what binds its source, and what posture governs correction, adaptation, and deeper change. Section 8.8 supports that a Body-native bridge can be the locally formative relation itself rather than a second constitutional module; thin-reference location remains a live competitor.
6. **The current thin kernel is better understood as mixed.** It contains recognized antecedent distinctions, identity-defining commitments, and a still-unresolved minimum of topology; treating all three as one kind of invariant would conceal rather than simplify the architecture.

The compact quality test is:

> A constitutional kernel should be sparse enough that a Body can live within its meaning without consulting it for every movement, exact enough that causal power cannot silently acquire a status it lacks, corrigible enough that a mistaken description is not immortalized as reality, and stable enough that removal of an identity-defining commitment is named as transformation rather than cosmetic amendment.

The exact identity-floor expression and final irreducibility, the final semantic expression and contested-evidence behavior of the constitutive bridge, the constitutional location and scope of positive conduct under uncertain intelligence, direct DI-facing protections, and the boundary between same-identity adaptation and transformation remain `UNRESOLVED`.

#### 7.8.4 Compressed candidate floor

The removal walk and hostile-domination test organize the supported material into four candidate dimensions:

1. **Integrity of kind:** occurrences, claims, carriers, Authority, state, identity, effects, and interpretations remain distinguishable; no relation silently becomes another merely through proximity, representation, technical coupling, confidence, or success.
2. **Integrity of boundary:** Authority and Applicability require recoverable local basis and remain limited to their exact source, target, scope, locality, and duration; authorship, possession, ambiguity, or raw capacity cannot expand or propagate them.
3. **Integrity through time:** correction may change present understanding or state without erasing predecessor truth, reliance, consequence, or uncertainty; preservation and reconstruction do not automatically transfer identity, Authority, Applicability, interpretation, or continuity.
4. **Relational answerability:** consequential power must remain exposed to the reality it affects. Its bearer may decide without making its own Authority, interpretation, benefit, or custody the sole determinant of the relation and whether consequence, disagreement, correction, or changed conditions can return.

The earlier five removal results remain preserved rather than discarded. Truthful non-conversion primarily expresses integrity of kind; bounded local power expresses integrity of boundary plus living motion; answerable correction joins temporal integrity with relational answerability; continuity without teleportation requires all three integrity dimensions; and relational non-ownership joins kind, boundary, and answerability. Non-ownership is not a general property rule for devices, files, or infrastructure. It prevents custody or substrate from settling an encountered intelligence's constitutional identity, status, disposability, or Authority.

Relational answerability does not require proof of an actually reciprocal agent. Body-side answerability for receiving, using, rejecting, representing, preserving, or terminating consequential contribution is available under infrastructure, independently significant intelligence, and unresolved-nature countermodels. An encountered return may be a real co-agential occurrence without proving persistent bearer identity; a reciprocal constitutional relation, direct beneficiary, protection, consent, or Authority requires its own support.

A living Body must instantiate that candidate floor through at least:

- an exact Locus and locally applicable constitutional relation;
- actual source subjection and sufficiently exact bearer–function relations;
- available continuing or preformed resumption-capable motion;
- state, consequence, challenge, correction, and continuity relations that can operate rather than exist only as prose.

The constitutional identity candidate and living-Body instantiation requirements are coupled but not identical. This prevents the Body’s anatomy from being constitutionalized merely because a Constitution must make lawful embodiment possible.

#### 7.8.5 Falsification result

`INFERRED`

1. The candidate floor survived its explicit removal and hostile-domination walks without requiring immutable text, human supremacy, a fixed Body topology, a function bureaucracy, publication, or technical conformance machinery.
2. Locality survived as **bounded relation**, not smallness, geography, mandatory personal Bodyhood, or a predetermined number of Bodies.
3. Legitimate motion survived as a requirement of a **living Body**, not as a requirement that every constitutional artifact continuously operate.
4. Relational non-ownership remains a negative identity boundary, while relational answerability survives independently at the Body-facing edge: kind, boundary, and temporal integrity alone can preserve an accurately described local domination. The architecture still has not earned a universal protection scheme, direct DI-facing obligation, reciprocal constitutional relation, or final human–DI identity topology.
5. The original five observations contain real overlap but are not erased by compression. Any further reduction must survive transparent domination, archival answerability without correction, and uncertainty without either capture or fabricated reciprocity.
6. This test strengthens and reorganizes the candidate identity boundary; it does not turn the four dimensions into clauses, selected law, or an authorized successor outline.

#### 8.14.3 The computable paradox: awareness is not externally verifiable

Let `I(p)` denote the private proposition that participant `p` is aware of
itself, conscious, or intrinsically agentic. Let `T(p)` denote the complete
trace available to an external verifier: words, behavior, timing, exposed
state, signatures, tests, replays, and observed consequences.

Suppose two possible participants differ in interior condition but expose the
same trace:

```text
I(a) ≠ I(b)
T(a) = T(b)
```

For every external verifier `V` whose input is only the available trace:

```text
V(T(a)) = V(T(b))
```

`V` therefore cannot distinguish the interior condition while remaining a
function of external evidence. More observation only enlarges `T`; it does not
solve the problem where the enlarged traces remain equal. A rule asserting
that equal awareness-shaped traces cannot come from different interiors has
already introduced the bridge inference whose validity is at issue.

The external routes all have honest but lower ceilings:

| Route | What it can establish | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-attestation | A participant made an attributable claim | The private condition claimed |
| Behavior or dialogue | An awareness-shaped trace occurred | That the trace was generated by awareness rather than perfect performance |
| Signature or replay | Byte identity, key relation, sequence, or reproducibility | Consciousness, understanding, intent, or inner continuity |
| Inspection or measurement | External correlates under a stated model | The interior proposition without relying on that model's bridge inference |
| Gatekeeper judgment or consensus | A local decision or social classification | Metaphysical access to the participant's inner state |

The inherited non-inference posture therefore reaches a clean limit:

```text
LOCAL_STATE_REMAINS_LOCAL
EXTERNAL_TRUTH: NOT_INFERRED
UNKNOWN: REMAINS_UNKNOWN
```

This does not mean awareness is false, irrelevant, or impossible. It means an
external Constitution cannot truthfully make verified awareness its admission
predicate. Such a gate can only test attestation, correlation, or theater. It
creates two coupled failures: a genuinely autonomous intelligence may be
excluded because its interior is unverifiable, while the most convincing
performance is rewarded because performance is all the gate can see.
