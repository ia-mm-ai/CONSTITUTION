### Capability, situated capacity, regulation, Authority, and consequence are different dimensions

`PRESSURE-SUPPORTED HISTORICAL COMPRESSION · NON-CONSTITUTIVE`

The prior `CAPACITY → IAMMAI / CAPABILITY → NOT_STANDING` classification no
longer survives the attached-corpus comparison. It reversed the historical
emphasis and made analytical dimensions look like successor jurisdictions.

The cleaner compression is:

```text
capability          = what a bearer or system can actually do
structural capacity = what a form leaves possible
situated capacity   = what this locality can presently bear or exercise
regulation          = how local conditions admit, bound, pause, redirect, or refuse motion
Authority           = the valid local basis on which a bounded motion may be chosen
consequence         = what actually changed
```

IAMMAI and IAMMAI-SYSTEM most strongly developed the **capability** side: typed
operations, integrity checking, witnessing, state movement, refusal, HOLD,
continuity support, and bounded execution. Their own seam law explicitly
refuses the conversion of usefulness, success, competence, centrality, or
dependence into Authority, governance, or sovereignty. This is a historical
emphasis, not a claim that every IAMMAI file has one function or that IAMMAI is
renamed `Capability`.

Reality-Model most strongly developed **dynamic situated capacity regulation**:
what one continuing Locality can presently admit, authorize, sustain, refuse,
change, remember, and bear as consequence under its exact state and relations.
It does not follow that Reality-Model owns capacity, that capability is outside
it, or that regulation itself supplies Authority.

The non-conversions are the durable seam:

```text
CAPABILITY ≠ CAPACITY
CAPACITY ≠ AUTHORITY
AUTHORITY ≠ EXECUTION
EXECUTION ≠ CONSEQUENCE
CONSEQUENCE ≠ SHARED TRUTH
```

A capable system may lack room or permission to act. A locality may retain
structural capacity while presently unable to exercise it. Valid Authority may
exist without execution; execution may occur without Authority; an effect may
occur without being admitted, understood, or shared. Regulation can preserve
these differences but cannot turn itself into the sovereign source of their
meaning.
