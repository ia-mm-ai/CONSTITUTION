# Consequence Boundary

`CONSEQUENCES/` carries actual effects or changed conditions attributable to the Body or its exact Locus. Consequence is not identical with intended effect, claimed effect, Act, occurrence, current state, evidence, interpretation, legitimacy, or settlement.

A consequence may occur without authorization, before observation, or before understanding. Recording it does not authorize, accept, settle, or legitimate it; make its cause certain; or place its source under Body jurisdiction. Evidence, observation, inference, and uncertainty concerning a consequence belong under `EPISTEMIC/` and must reference rather than absorb the consequence.

Every consequence record must identify its exact target, actual local change, time or interval, source where knowable, attribution basis, related occurrence or external impact, legitimacy posture, uncertainty, predecessor, and any supported currentness effect. Later correction or reattribution preserves the predecessor account rather than silently rewriting it.

`INDEX.json` is the current navigation surface. Its existence creates no consequence, occurrence, legitimacy, remedy, or settlement.
