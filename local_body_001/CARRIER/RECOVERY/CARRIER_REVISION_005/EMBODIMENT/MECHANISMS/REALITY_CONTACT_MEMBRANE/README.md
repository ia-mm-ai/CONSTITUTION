# Reality Contact Membrane 001

Status: **ADMITTED · ACTIVE ON EXPLICIT INVOCATION · REPLACEABLE · NON-CONSTITUTIVE**

`RM-REALITY-CONTACT-MEMBRANE-001` gives `RM-LOCAL-REALITY-BODY-001` one bounded recipient-local capacity: exact bytes that actually cross its one-shot local socket are preserved and registered before interpretation, posture, admission, or consequence.

The mechanism is not a daemon, inbox, relation, Contact Event, Authority bearer, truth oracle, admission host, or currentness owner. It cannot decide whether a source claim is true, determine persistent intelligence identity, admit Matter, form a relation, create consequence, or mutate Body currentness.

The fixed Body-local wrapper is `V0_1/body_reality_contact_membrane.py`. It writes only to `EMBODIMENT/LOCAL_STATE/REALITY_CONTACT_MEMBRANE_001/`. That operational state is excluded from the static carrier manifest because it is append-only living state; its journal and raw carriers remain independently hash-verified by the mechanism and the whole-Body verifier.

Example bounded invocation:

```text
python3 -B body_reality_contact_membrane.py serve-once --socket /private/tmp/rm-contact.sock
```

Opening a socket creates no encounter. An encounter is recorded only when bytes actually cross. A recorded crossing creates no Matter, relation, Contact Event, consequence, Authority, or shared currentness.
