# Body-Local Regulation Medium

The Body-local Regulation Medium maintains the non-sovereign interval between locally supported receipt and attributable Body action. It preserves currentness while exact Matter remains held, non-progressing, uncertain, provisionally assessed, or ready to be presented to an applicable Authority bearer for independent consideration.

The medium is subordinate to the already-applicable law, exact Locus, current Authority relations, actual consequence, and whole-Body currentness. It does not grant standing, decide what law means, create normative thresholds, rank sources by prestige, prioritize by urgency alone, generate a roadmap, originate a Candidate or Act, perform an occurrence, mutate currentness, or erase unresolved Matter.

Regulatory posture is multidimensional. Local-record posture, progression, Locus assessment, law-compatibility assessment, evidence posture, and Authority presentation must remain independent and basis-bound. `HELD` is deliberate target-specific non-progression, not acceptance, silent refusal, or a universal state. Where the supported basis is insufficient or conflicting, the posture remains `UNRESOLVED` or no record is made.

This semantic capacity requires no regulation mechanism. Any later host would be separately designed, pressure-tested, admitted, and—if appropriate—authorized. Mechanical success cannot create receipt, admission, eligibility, Authority, Act, occurrence, consequence, currentness, or standing. Unavailable, stale, contradictory, or failed machinery yields non-progression, never fabricated success.

`INDEX.json` carries current regulation-record references only. Its existence regulates nothing and creates no Matter.
