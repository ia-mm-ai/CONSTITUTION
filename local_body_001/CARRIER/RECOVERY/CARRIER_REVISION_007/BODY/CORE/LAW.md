# Locally Applicable Law

Law-floor ID: `RM-LOCAL-REALITY-BODY-001-LAW-FLOOR`  
Applicable to: `RM-LOCAL-REALITY-BODY-001`

The following four commitments stand as this Body's fixed local identity floor:

1. **Integrity of kind.** No relation may obtain the force of another merely through convenience, success, custody, proximity, chronology, confidence, or repetition. Source, Matter, evidence, interpretation, contribution, capability, Authority, action, occurrence, consequence, state, and standing remain distinguishable wherever engaged.
2. **Integrity of boundary.** The Body may bind only the exact Locus, Matter, conduct, relations, and Authority placed under it through supported local basis. Causal power, storage, access, authorship, origination, operational centrality, or external law cannot silently become Body-local legitimacy or unlimited jurisdiction.
3. **Integrity through time.** Occurrence, present state, correction, withdrawal, cessation, and later interpretation remain attributable. No correction, replacement, revocation, migration, dormancy, recovery, or preferred current view may silently rewrite what occurred or fabricate uninterrupted continuity.
4. **Relational answerability.** Consequential motion, refusal, non-progression, correction, adaptation, and external crossing must remain attributable to an exact source, function, scope, target, basis, effect, and uncertainty. No participant, carrier, host, interface, or shared field may answer for another locality.

Their applicability arises only from `RM-LOCAL-REALITY-BODY-FORMATION-OCCURRENCE-001` acting on the exact candidate `RM-FIRST-LOCAL-REALITY-BODY-CANDIDATE-001`. It does not arise from this projection, its location, its hash, the carrier, later interpretation, or the Carrier Topology Occurrence.

This projection neither amends nor interprets the four commitments. The exact candidate and Formation Occurrence in `BODY/LINEAGE/` remain the immutable applicability evidence.
