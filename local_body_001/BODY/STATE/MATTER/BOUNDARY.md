# Matter Boundary

`BODY/STATE/MATTER/` carries exact subjects placed under bounded Body-local handling through a supported local basis. It does not carry the world, everything noticed, every nearby file, or every object that may be useful.

External pressure or source object, local receipt record, referenced content, admitted Body-local Matter, regulation subject, epistemic relevance, and standing remain distinct. A local record may identify an external source without importing, adopting, owning, or making true its content.

Every Matter record must identify its exact subject, source or reference, receiving or admission basis, local target and scope, predecessor, uncertainty, explicit non-effects, and any exact regulation-record relation that actually stands. Unsupported receipt or admission remains absent or `UNRESOLVED`; urgency, repetition, authorship, custody, readability, proximity, or storage cannot supply the missing basis.

`INDEX.json` is the current navigation surface. Its existence admits no Matter, source, claim, intelligence, relation, or consequence.
