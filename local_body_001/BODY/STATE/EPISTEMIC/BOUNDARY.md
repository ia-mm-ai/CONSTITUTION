# Epistemic Boundary

Intelligence may emerge through relation and persist locally as changed capacity, memory, artifacts, decisions, consequence observations, and future relational posture.

The Body may carry exact epistemic residues that have been locally admitted. It cannot thereby contain or own intelligence itself, another participant, another participant's knowledge, a shared field, shared truth, or another locality's currentness.

Source relation, observation, evidence, inference, working interpretation, uncertainty, consequence observation, actual consequence, and standing remain distinct. An actual consequence belongs under `BODY/STATE/CONSEQUENCE/`; epistemic material concerning it belongs here. Recording or indexing either does not upgrade it into the other. Epistemic change does not by itself create identity, Participation, Authority, law, relation, consent, consequence, or external standing.

`INDEX.json` is a receiving and distinction surface only. Its existence admits nothing. Any later entry requires exact local uptake, attribution, classification, effect ceiling, and uncertainty.

No workspace, Recovery corpus, historical corpus, conversation, quotation, research collection, DI output, or relationally emergent intelligence is admitted by this boundary or the present empty index.
